Description

This makes your house smell so heavenly! It is a great brunch item.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ sticks of butter, softened
  • 1 ½ cups sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 tsp. vanilla
  • 1 5/8 cup flour
  • 1 tsp. baking powder
  • ¼ tsp. salt
  • 1 ½ cups blueberries - coated in flour
  • ½ cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1 tsp. cinnamon

Directions

  1. Grease a 13x9 pan
  2. Cream butter and sugar
  3. Add eggs, one at a time
  4. Mix in sour cream and vanilla
  5. In a separate bowl, combine flour, baking powder, and salt
  6. Add flour mixture to the other bowl.
  7. Gently fold in the blueberries
  8. Spread mixture into the 13x9 pan
  9. In a small bowl, combine the brown sugar and cinnamon, then sprinkle over the top of the batter
  10. Bake at 350 degrees for 40 minutes or until toothpick inserted in middle comes out clean
